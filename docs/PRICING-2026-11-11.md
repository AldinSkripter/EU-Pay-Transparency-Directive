# Prices and subscription terms — 11 November 2026

New B2B orders, excluding VAT:

| Plan | Employees | Monthly payment | Annual payment / minimum total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare | 1–249 | €119 | €1,428 |
| Growth | 250–999 | €249 | €2,988 |
| Scale | 1,000–2,500 | €499 | €5,988 |

Both payment frequencies have a minimum term of 12 months from the paid subscription start. Monthly payment means 12 monthly instalments in the first year. Annual payment has no additional discount. Subscriptions renew until cancelled: monthly thereafter for monthly billing, yearly for annual billing. Regular cancellation takes effect at the first anniversary or the end of the current billing period, whichever is later. Statutory extraordinary termination rights are unaffected.

Checkout requires explicit consent to version `pay-2026-11-11-12m`. A company document records consent time, user, prices, employee limit and payment frequency per order. Existing orders are not repriced or given a new minimum term retroactively; legacy employee limits remain available. No schema migration is required.

The account shows the agreed terms and provides cancellation preview plus explicit confirmation. Stripe receives a future `cancel_at`; access remains active until the provider cancellation event. The linked Stripe portal handles invoices/payment methods with cancellation and plan changes disabled for these new contracts. Demo cancellation simulates expiry locally.

Before taking real payments, create six recurring EUR prices in Stripe for the amounts above and save/verify their IDs in the owner payment settings. Existing Stripe price amounts cannot simply be edited. Checkout independently checks the selected price and rejects an old price before creating an order. No live Stripe products or prices were created as part of this local change. Ensure any separately enabled public Stripe portal also cannot bypass the agreed cancellation process. Provider dashboard changes and statutory/manual exceptions require operator handling.
